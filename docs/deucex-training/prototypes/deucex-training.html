What Resonance does not do

Read this
It is not a medical device and it makes no health claim. If something feels wrong physically, that is a conversation with a doctor, not with an app.
Camera heart rate is a relative signal for training. It is not a clinical heart rate variability measurement and it should never be read as one.
There is no readiness score and no recovery score. Nothing here tells you whether to play.
Camera frames are read once on your phone and never stored or sent anywhere.
Include heart data in the coach linkOff by default. Marko sees your matches, shortlists and patterns, never this.

Why a personal rate

Everyone has a breathing rate at which the heart rate swing is largest, usually between four and seven breaths a minute. At that rate the breath and the heart reinforce each other instead of fighting. The test finds yours once, then you stop thinking about it.

Sixteen

The gap between points is about seventy percent of a match and the only part of it you fully control. Sixteen drills the four phases of the routine, then measures what you actually do out there.

Median 18.4s · 12% variation Sample set Pro

Rehearse the routine

90 seconds

Four phases, five times through. Stand up and do it, with a racquet if one is to hand. It is rote learning, and that is the point: at 4-5 in the third you should not be deciding what to do next.

Mirror a practice set

The measurement

Prop the phone on the fence, or hand it to whoever is watching. Sixteen does not follow the ball. It works out where the points ended and times the gaps between them.

What leaves the microphoneNothing. The sound is analysed on this phone and never recorded, stored or sent. Only the times of the ball strikes survive the session, and they stay here.

What Sixteen does not do

Read this
It does not judge your technique, score the match or tell you that you played badly. It times gaps, and that is all it times.
Shorter is not better. The number that matters is how steady you are, not how fast. The 25-second clock is the only hard limit here.
Listening cannot tell your court from the one next to it, or a rally from someone hitting against a wall. Check the point count before you trust a set.
No audio is recorded or kept, in any mode, at any point. There is nothing to delete because nothing is written.
Include the routine pattern in the coach linkOn by default. Marko sees the sentence and the count, never the set, never the score.
