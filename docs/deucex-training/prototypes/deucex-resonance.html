What Resonance does not do

Read this
It is not a medical device and it makes no health claim. If something feels wrong physically, that is a conversation with a doctor, not with an app.
Camera heart rate is a relative signal for training. It is not a clinical heart rate variability measurement and it should never be read as one.
There is no readiness score and no recovery score. Nothing here tells you whether to play.
Camera frames are read once on your phone and never stored or sent anywhere.
Include heart data in the coach linkOff by default. Marko sees your matches, shortlists and patterns, never this.

Why a personal rate

Everyone has a breathing rate at which the heart rate swing is largest, usually between four and seven breaths a minute. At that rate the breath and the heart reinforce each other instead of fighting. The test finds yours once, then you stop thinking about it.
