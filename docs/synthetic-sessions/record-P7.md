## Notes on the kit itself

1. The handover assumes the prototype fits the player. A WTA player saw "ATP singles ranking", initials LN and an Austrian patron list, and said "this is not me" before task 1 began; every later answer carried "but this is not my list". The kit needs a stated fallback for players outside the ATP band, or a note that these sessions read as mismatch data.
2. Task 3 wording ("say out loud what you'd actually put into this note") is leading for a player who never speaks her notes; it assumes voice is the capture mode. Her refusal is the finding, but the script gave no typed alternative to test, so we cannot say whether she would write it into the app.
3. Task 1 asks what the three boxes mean, then Task 7 is only described, not shown. For a Stage 3 player with a full team, the runway and patron tiles are somebody else's job; the "three answers" premise was not tested so much as declined.
4. The moderator broke the rule at 00:22 by explaining the Neumayer sample before she had tried the interface, and at 00:44 by asking her to tap after she had said she would not. Both were understandable but contaminate task 6.
5. "Runway", "patrons" and "Inside Track" did not translate; she rendered them as "money weeks", "Patreon" and a question. The pricing comparison to "one restring" landed (she priced it in yen instantly); "two hotel nights" did not, because her team books hotels. The session ran to 01:04 mainly because of the 14 s and 9 s silences, which were worth it.
