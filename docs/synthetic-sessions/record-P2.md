## Notes on the kit itself

1. The Arya prototype is the wrong stimulus for a WTA Stage 1 player. Her first eight seconds were spent working out that it was not for her, and that framing sat under every task answer afterwards ("pretending", "a bloke from Austria"). The kit should say what to do when there is no matching persona, rather than defaulting to the ATP Stage 2 build.
2. Task 2's "would you enter it" assumes the shortlist is one the player could enter. A men's CH 75 is not, so her answer was hypothetical from the start, which weakens the cost-to-prize finding. The finding that she decides on points and flight rather than prize still stands, because it came from discovery before the screen.
3. The A$49 "one restring" comparison did not translate for a player who strings her own frames; she re-priced it as three restrings on the spot. The comparison should be checked against the player's own stringing answer from D5 before it is read.
4. The moderator explained "ratio" after she asked at 00:24. He did not, which was correct, but at 00:39 he said "it's just a draft" before she tapped approve, which pre-empted the prediction the task is meant to test. Her "so it hasn't gone?" is therefore weaker evidence than it looks.
5. Timing: the session ran to 01:02 with a player who had flagged a hard stop at 00:03. The pricing block started at 00:46 rather than 00:45, which was fine, but the unprompted Settings exploration at 00:44 (which produced the best WTA finding of the session) ate the slack. The kit should say whether unprompted exploration is allowed to eat into pricing time.
