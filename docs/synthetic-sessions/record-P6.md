## Notes on the kit itself

1. Task 1 wording ("these three boxes") tells the player there are three things to read before they have looked; Nikola had already tapped the ranking chart, and the wording pulled him back down to the tiles. The unprompted first touch (ranking chart, points to defend) is the truer data and the script slightly overwrote it.
2. Task 6 assumes the player will predict that nothing leaves the app, but the button reads "Approve and publish" and the send time sits next to it. A player who reads the label predicts a send. The kit's success criterion and the prototype's copy disagree; that is a copy decision, not a player failure.
3. The pricing anchors did not translate for a Stage 3 player: one restring is trivial when you string four frames a week, and "two hotel nights" is nothing when Challenger main-draw hotels are hospitality. Both comparisons drew a laugh rather than a reaction to the price. AUD also forced him to convert every figure; the kit should let the moderator read prices in euros for European players.
4. Discovery question 2 ("the last flight or hotel you paid for") assumes the player paid. For a parent-managed player the honest answer is "I didn't", and the runway probe lands on the wrong person. Consider a branch: "who paid, and do they know the runway?"
5. Moderator broke the no-narration rule once: at 00:21, when Nikola said "money weeks", Manu explained runway before he had finished reading the tile. His later runway answers are contaminated.
