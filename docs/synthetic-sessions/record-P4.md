## Notes on the kit itself

1. Handing a WTA #520 the Arya (ATP male, Austrian, A$) prototype coloured the whole task block. Her first words were "this is a man", and two of her three confusions (currency, ranking label) were mismatches rather than product findings. A WTA skin with EUR would have been cheap and would have let task 1 test the premise cleanly.
2. Task 3 asks the player to "say out loud" a note in front of the founder, in her second language, after she has just told us in discovery that her real notes are four pages of German at 1 a.m. The task measured whether she'd perform for a room, not whether she'd talk to software. A private-recording variant (moderator steps out, player records alone) would be closer.
3. Task 6's read-aloud says "Go ahead and approve this" and the button says "Approve and publish". She predicted a send because the word publish was on the button, then the kit scored her as not understanding the gate. The finding may be about the button label, not the player, and the scoring row cannot tell the difference.
4. The pricing comparison "A$149, roughly two nights in the kind of hotel you'd usually book" did not translate: for a W35 to W75 player in Europe it is three nights, and she corrected the anchor before answering. The anchors should be set from the player's own numbers given a minute earlier, not from a fixed line.
5. Timing: discovery ran to 00:18 as planned, but the moderator explained the "why" paragraph during task 2 (00:24) before she had found it, against the "do not narrate" rule, and the session ran to 01:01 because the sponsor question at 00:37 was worth following. The 8 percent question was asked at 00:52 with the payout table showing a flat 10 percent on screen, which she noticed and which muddied the answer.
