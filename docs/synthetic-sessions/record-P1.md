## Notes on the kit itself

1. Task 1 reads "these three boxes" but the first thing a Stage 1 player sees is a Stage 2 hero for a different person; the first two minutes went to "who is Arya" and "where is my UTR" before the tiles were reached. A Stage 1 Arya state, or at least a Stage 1 hero, is needed for junior sessions.
2. Task 2's Challenger Poznań assumes a player who can enter Challengers. For a #1,380 the question "would you enter it" is answered by acceptance rules, not by cost; he engaged only by role-playing Arya. The shortlist bias per stage (M15/M25) should be in the stimulus.
3. The pricing block converts to hotel nights and restrings, but this player prices everything in złoty and in his mother's money; "whose money" should come first, not fourth, for a funded junior, because every earlier answer was really about her.
4. Moderator broke the silence rule once at 00:36 (Task 5), offering "you don't have to answer" after about five seconds; the courtesy answer ("maybe at 500") followed directly from that. At 00:11 the silence was held correctly and produced the finding.
5. "Runway" and "patrons" did not translate for an 18-year-old thinking in Polish; he asked whether runway meant money. The fee question (A$600 from supporters) was a hypothetical he could not inhabit, so the answer is close to noise. Session ran to 62 minutes.
